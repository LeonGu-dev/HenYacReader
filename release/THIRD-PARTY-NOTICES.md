# Third-party components

HenYacReader is derived from YACReader by Luis Ángel San Martín Rodríguez under GPL v3; the license is included as COPYING.txt. Original source attribution and notices remain in the repository.

This Windows build uses Qt 6.9.3, 7-Zip, PDFium and OpenSSL, plus the libraries retained from YACReader. Their source, notices and license information are available at their respective upstream projects:

- Qt: https://www.qt.io/licensing/ and https://code.qt.io/
- 7-Zip: https://www.7-zip.org/license.txt
- PDFium: https://pdfium.googlesource.com/pdfium/
- OpenSSL: https://www.openssl.org/source/ and https://github.com/openssl/openssl
- QsLog: its license is included as QsLog-LICENSE.txt.
- Other compiled third-party sources and notices are retained in the repository's third_party directory.

Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable is included as its original vc_redist.x64.exe installer; running it is subject to Microsoft's displayed terms.
